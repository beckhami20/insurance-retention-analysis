# Insurance Customer Retention Analysis

## Project Overview

Customer retention is an important part of the insurance industry because losing existing policyholders can affect profitability, growth, and long-term customer relationships.

This project analyzes insurance policyholder data to identify patterns and factors associated with customer retention and cancellation. The goal is to better understand why customers may leave and identify opportunities where retention efforts could be most effective.

## Business Question

What factors are associated with insurance policy cancellations, and how can those insights be used to improve customer retention?

## Project Goals

- Analyze customer and policy characteristics associated with retention and cancellation.
- Identify patterns among customers who cancel their policies.
- Determine which factors may indicate an increased risk of cancellation.
- Develop visualizations that clearly communicate retention trends.
- Translate the analysis into practical business recommendations.
